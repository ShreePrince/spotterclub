Our coaches

Qualified. Insured. Great at video.

Every Spotter coach is a qualified personal trainer who has been trained to coach well over video.

Qualified and insured

Coaches hold a recognised personal training qualification and carry professional insurance.

Trained for video

Coaching on camera is a skill. Our coaches learn to cue, correct and motivate through a screen.

Specialisms for every goal

Strength, mobility, pre and postnatal, conditioning and event training. Find a coach who fits you.

What to expect

A coach who is paying attention

They start with you

Your coach reviews your goals, injuries and equipment before planning anything, then keeps the plan current as you progress.

They are easy to reach

Message your coach with questions, photos or wins. They reply, and they notice when you have gone quiet.

They keep notes

After each session your coach records what went well and what is next, so nothing gets lost between sessions.

They look after your health

Client health information is protected, and coaches are trained to work safely around injuries and conditions.
