About us

Personal training that fits real life

We started Spotter because good coaching should not depend on where you live or how close you are to a gym.

Most people who want to get stronger do not fail for lack of effort. They fail because the plan does not fit their week, nobody notices when they slip, and nobody corrects their form. A good coach fixes all three.

Spotter puts that coach in your pocket. You meet over live video, follow a plan built around your goals and equipment, and message each other between sessions. It is the part of personal training that works, without the commute.

What we believe

Our principles

People first

Your coach should know your name, your history and your goals. Technology is there to support that relationship, not replace it.

Safe and professional

Qualified, insured coaches and careful handling of health information are the baseline, not a feature.

Built for consistency

Small, repeatable habits beat heroic effort. We design everything to help you keep showing up.

Two apps, one platform

For clients and for coaches

Clients get a simple app for sessions, plans and messaging. Coaches get their own app for running sessions, managing clients and keeping notes.

Want to talk?

Whether you want to train with us, coach with us or just ask a question, we would love to hear from you.
