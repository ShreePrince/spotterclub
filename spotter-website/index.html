Why Spotter

Everything a good gym session has, minus the gym

Accountability, feedback on your form and a plan that adapts. That is what makes training stick.

Live coaching

Train one to one over video. Your coach watches your technique, adjusts the session and keeps you moving.

A plan that fits your week

A weekly programme of live sessions and home workouts, built around your goals, your kit and your schedule.

Support between sessions

Message your coach with questions, photos or wins. Check in when life gets busy and get back on track.

Train anywhere

A living room, a garage or a hotel gym. Sessions work with the space and equipment you actually have.

Progress you can see

Track your sessions, your lifts and your consistency so you know your effort is paying off.

Built around you

New to lifting, returning after a break or working around an injury? Your coach starts where you are.

How it works

From sign-up to your first session in four steps

  1. Tell us your goals

    Pick what you want to work on, from getting stronger to moving without pain.

  2. Choose your coach

    Browse our coaches and pick the one you would like to train with.

  3. Book your first session

    Start with an induction call so your coach can learn about you, then book live sessions that suit you.

  4. Train and progress

    Follow your weekly plan, message your coach and watch your numbers climb.
