Good to know

Questions about plans

Can I change plan later?

Yes. You can move up or down from your account at any time, and the change is recorded against your subscription.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans run month to month and you can cancel whenever you like.

What if I miss a session?

You can reschedule from the app. Message your coach if life gets in the way and they will help you get back on track.
